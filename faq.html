Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our surf camp, booking, and your stay in Morocco.

Absolutely no experience is required. Our coaches work with complete beginners as well as intermediate and advanced surfers. Choose the package that matches your level — Surf Coaching (beginner & intermediate) or Surf Guiding (intermediate & advanced) — and we’ll tailor the experience to your goals.

Think light and surf-ready: swimwear, a sun-protective rash guard or t-shirt, sunscreen, a towel, flip-flops, and a light layer for the evenings. Everything else — boards, wetsuits, bed linen and towels — is provided at the camp.

Winter (October to March) brings consistent, larger swells to the point breaks, ideal for intermediate and advanced surfers. Summer (April to September) offers smaller, playful waves and warmer water, perfect for beginners and longboarders. With 300+ sunny days a year, there’s always a good time to come.

Yes. The closest airport is Agadir — Al Massira Airport (AGA), about a 45-minute drive away. We strongly recommend booking our airport transfer for a hassle-free arrival. Just let us know your flight details when you book.

Of course! Many of our guests travel solo. Our family-like atmosphere, communal meals and group activities make it easy to meet fellow surfers from around the world. It’s the perfect way to combine surfing with new friendships.

Booking is confirmed with a 30% deposit (non-refundable). You can reschedule for free up to 7 days before arrival. The balance is usually paid on arrival or as agreed at booking. Contact us for full details for your specific dates.
