The Heart of Moroccan Surf

Welcome to Tamraght, a Berber village perfectly positioned between the mountains and the Atlantic.

Why Tamraght?

The Relaxed, Authentic Heart of the Surf Scene

Tamraght offers the perfect balance: immediate access to world-class waves for all levels, while maintaining a peaceful village charm away from the main tourist hustle.

With its friendly locals, rooftop cafes, and stunning sunsets over the Atlantic, Tamraght isn’t just a place to surf — it’s a place to live the surf lifestyle.

How to Get Here

45 Minutes From Agadir Airport

The closest airport is Agadir — Al Massira Airport (AGA), which is served by many international airlines.

From the airport, it’s a scenic 45-minute drive to our surf camp in Tamraght. We highly recommend booking an airport transfer with us for a hassle-free arrival. Taxis are also readily available at the airport.

Weather & Surf Seasons

300 Days of Sunshine a Year

Morocco boasts over 300 days of sunshine a year — there’s almost always a wave waiting for you.

Winter (Oct — Mar)

The prime surf season with consistent, large swells hitting the point breaks. Perfect for intermediate and advanced surfers.

Summer (Apr — Sep)

Smaller, playful waves and warm water. Ideal for beginners, longboarders, and those looking to improve in fun conditions.

Beyond the Waves

Explore Southern Morocco

When the wind picks up, the adventure doesn’t stop. Discover the region around the camp.

Paradise Valley

A stunning oasis in the mountains, perfect for a day trip of cliff jumping and relaxing.

Taghazout Village

Explore the vibrant fishing village, with its cafes, shops, and lively atmosphere.

Get lost in one of Morocco’s largest markets, a feast for the senses with spices, crafts, and more.
