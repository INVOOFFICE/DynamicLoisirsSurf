A Taste of Taghazout: From Fresh Fish to Rooftop Cafes

Tamraght's vibrant neighbor, Taghazout, is a feast for the senses.

Just a short drive from our camp in Tamraght lies Taghazout — a classic Moroccan fishing village that has grown into one of the most iconic surf towns in the world. Between the relaxed vibes and famous point breaks, it’s a feast for the senses.

Where to Eat

Follow the smell of grilling fish toward the beach and you’ll find the best of Taghazout. Lunch here is a simple formula: catch of the day, grilled over coals, served with bread and salad. It doesn’t get better after a morning in the water.

  • Fresh fish grills near the beachfront — sardines, sea bream and more.
  • Berber omelettes in the morning before the surf kicks in.
  • Fresh juices and smoothies to cool down mid-day.
  • Mint tea everywhere, all the time.

Rooftop Cafes & Sunset Views

As the sun drops behind the Atlantic, Taghazout’s rooftops come alive. Pull up a cushion, order a tea, and watch the surfers catch the last waves of the day at the point. It’s the perfect way to end a day of surf, food, and friends.

Shop Like a Local

Wander the narrow streets and you’ll find surf shops, craft stalls, and Berber goods — from leather babouche slippers to woven rugs and silver jewelry. A little browsing with a tea in hand is its own kind of sport.

"The best way to see Taghazout is slow. Breakfast late, surf the point, eat fish, drink tea, watch the sunset. Repeat."

Stay with us in Tamraght and Taghazout is right on your doorstep. Check our packages or get in touch to plan your trip.
