A Perfect Day Trip: Oasis-Hopping in Paradise Valley

Beyond the waves, a different kind of water awaits — the emerald pools of Paradise Valley.

You’ve spent the week chasing waves, riding the green walls of Anchor Point and getting that well-earned arm pump. Now it’s time to swap saltwater for something a little more serene. A short drive inland from Tamraght, the Atlas Mountains hide one of Morocco’s most stunning natural wonders.

What Is Paradise Valley?

Paradise Valley is a gorge carved by centuries of mountain water, famous for its series of crystal-clear emerald pools, natural rock slides, and palm-shaded terraces. The water is cool, fresh, and a welcome change of temperature after the Atlantic — perfect for a float between cliff jumps.

The Perfect Day Trip

We usually set off mid-morning, once the morning surf session is done and the sun is high. A guide will lead the way along the mountain paths, and by midday you’ll be swimming in a pool that looks straight out of a postcard.

  • Cliff jumping: pick your height — there’s a ledge for everyone.
  • Natural rock slides: a gentle ride into the deep blue pools.
  • Picnic on the rocks: our packed lunch tastes even better with a mountain view.
  • Photography: bring your camera, the colors here are unreal.

Good to Know

Bring water shoes (the rocks are smooth and slippery), a towel, and sun cream. There’s a famous restaurant with views over the valley where we often stop for mint tea on the way back. The whole trip takes about half a day, making it the perfect relaxed break in the middle of your surf week.

Our tip: schedule Paradise Valley for the windiest day of your stay. When the morning swell is blown out, the pools are calling.
