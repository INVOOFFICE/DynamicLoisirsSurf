About Dynamic Loisirs

Your home for an unforgettable, all-inclusive surf holiday in Morocco.

Our Story

Passion for Surfing, Love for Morocco

Located in the heart of Tamraght, Taghazout Bay, we offer the perfect blend of thrilling waves, authentic Moroccan culture, and a friendly, family-like atmosphere.

Whether you’re a beginner eager to catch your first wave or an expert seeking the best breaks, our certified instructors and local guides will ensure you have an unforgettable surf adventure.

Our mission is to share our passion for surfing and our love for Morocco with every guest. We believe in creating a welcoming environment where everyone feels like part of the family. Come join us and experience the magic of surfing in Tamraght.

Why Surf With Dynamic Loisirs?

We’re More Than Just a Surf Camp

We combine passion, professionalism, and the warmth of local culture for your gateway to an unforgettable Moroccan surf adventure.

Prime Surf Location

Situated in the heart of Tamraght, with easy access to world-famous spots like Taghazout and Anchor Point.

Expert Local Guides

Our certified and experienced local instructors know the tides, swells, and secret spots to ensure you score the best waves.

Authentic Moroccan Hospitality

Experience a true family-like atmosphere. We share our culture, our food, and our passion for surfing with every guest.

For All Skill Levels

From complete beginners to advanced surfers, our coaching and guiding packages are tailored to your personal goals.
