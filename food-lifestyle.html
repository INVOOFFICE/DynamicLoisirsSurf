A Taste of Morocco

Fresh, Local, Traditional

Every meal at Dynamic Loisirs is a celebration of fresh, local ingredients and traditional Moroccan cuisine. Our in-house chefs prepare daily breakfasts, packed lunches for the beach, and delicious family-style dinners that will refuel you after a long day in the water.

  • Vegetarian, vegan & gluten-free diets welcome
  • Daily breakfast, packed lunch & dinner
  • Fresh fish, local produce & Moroccan spices
Signature Dishes

Moments Worth Remembering

Tagine Thursdays

A traditional slow-cooked stew, rich with spices, meat, and vegetables. A Moroccan classic!

Fresh Fish Fridays

Enjoying the catch of the day, grilled to perfection, straight from the local fishing boats.

Berber Omelettes

A hearty and delicious breakfast to fuel your day of surfing, cooked in a traditional tagine.

Diets & Preferences

Everybody Eats Well Here

We believe everyone should enjoy delicious food. Our kitchen is happy to cater to vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free diets. Just let us know your dietary requirements when you book your stay, and we’ll take care of the rest.
