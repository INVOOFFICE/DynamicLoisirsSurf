About This Package

Planning a trip with your friends, surf club, or colleagues? We offer custom all-inclusive packages for groups of 5 or more. We can tailor the experience to your group’s skill level and interests.

Whether you’re all beginners, a mix of intermediate and advanced surfers, or anything in between, enjoy the camaraderie of a group trip while experiencing the best of our surf camp in Morocco. Contact us to create a personalized surf adventure for your crew, including private coaching, guiding, and villa booking options.
