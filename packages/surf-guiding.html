About This Package

For the experienced surfer who wants to discover the best waves Morocco has to offer. Our local guides will take you to the best spots based on the day’s conditions, away from the crowds.

This all-inclusive package is designed for intermediate and advanced surfers ready to explore the world-class point breaks and hidden gems around Tamraght and Taghazout. If you’re comfortable in the water and want to maximize your wave count, our surf guiding package is the ultimate Moroccan surfing experience.
