From Beginner to Pro

Our Teaching Philosophy

Personalized coaching in a safe, fun, and supportive environment. We keep our groups small to ensure you get the individual attention you need to progress.

Beginner level

Catch Your First Wave

  • No experience needed — we start on the sand
  • Paddling techniques
  • How to stand up (pop-up)
  • Board control
  • Ocean safety
Intermediate level

Ride the Green Waves

  • Paddling out the back
  • Catching unbroken green waves
  • Basic turns (forehand & backhand)
  • Wave selection
  • Surf etiquette
Advanced level

Master Your Technique

  • Advanced maneuvers & cutbacks
  • Wave reading
  • Generating speed
  • Video analysis with detailed feedback
  • Competition preparation

No Hidden Extras

Quality Equipment Included

We provide a wide range of surfboards (soft-tops, longboards, shortboards) and high-quality wetsuits to keep you comfortable in the water. All equipment rental is included in our all-inclusive packages, so you don’t have to worry about a thing.

Safety First, Always

Your safety is our top priority. All our instructors are certified lifeguards and hold ISA (International Surfing Association) coaching certifications. We choose the safest spots for our lessons and maintain a low student-to-instructor ratio.
